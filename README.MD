TagMedic is a smart, automatic cleanup tool for your music collection. If you have a messy folder full of random audio files, downloaded music videos, and duplicate tracks with missing names, TagMedic fixes it for you. You point it at your folder, and it handles the rest—leaving you with a perfectly organized, fully tagged digital library.

### [+] WHAT IT DOES

* **Turns Videos into Music:** Automatically finds video files (like `.mp4` or `.mkv`) and extracts the high-quality audio from them so you can listen to them as regular music tracks.
* **Fixes Missing Information:** Scrubs messy file names (removing clutter like "[Official Music Video]") and quietly asks Apple iTunes for the correct Song Name, Artist, Album, and Year.
* **Adds Album Art:** Downloads the official, high-resolution album cover for every song and embeds it directly into the file so it looks great on your phone or media player.
* **Cleans Up Clutter:** Scans your entire collection for exact duplicates. It safely moves the extra copies to your Recycle Bin so they stop wasting your hard drive space.
* **Sets Itself Up:** If your computer is missing the tools it needs to run, TagMedic detects the missing pieces and automatically downloads and installs them for you before it starts.

### [+] HOW IT WORKS

```text

        |
        +---> TagMedic identifies what is audio and what is video.

        +---> It pulls the audio track out of the video files.

        +---> It scans the whole batch and throws away exact duplicates.
        |
        +---> It downloads the correct names, tags, and cover art from Apple.
        |
        +---> It embeds the data directly into your files.
        |
        v
 [ PERFECTLY ORGANIZED MUSIC LIBRARY ]
```
